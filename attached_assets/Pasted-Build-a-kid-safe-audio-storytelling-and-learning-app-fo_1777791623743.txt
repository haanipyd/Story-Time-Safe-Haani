Build a kid-safe audio storytelling and learning app for toddlers aged 1-5 years,
inspired by Yoto and Tonies. The app is screen-light and audio-first.
Toddlers tap large picture cards to play stories.

TARGET USER
- Primary user: child aged 1-5 (cannot read; taps pictures)
- Parent does the initial setup but doesn't need ongoing PIN-protected access

CORE PRINCIPLE: SCREEN-LIGHT
The kid-facing screens are intentionally simple — large picture cards,
minimal text, no navigation deeper than 2 taps. The screen exists only to start audio;
the audio is the experience. Once playing, screen dims and shows just a play/pause
button and the story illustration.

ONBOARDING (first-time only, very short)
Three steps, one screen each:

1. Welcome → "What's your child's name?" (text input)
2. "How old is [name]?" (age picker: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 — large round buttons)
3. "What does [name] enjoy?" (multi-select preference picker)
   Options shown as large illustrated tiles, pick 3-6:
   - Bedtime stories
   - Adventure tales
   - Animal stories
   - Songs and rhymes
   - Mythology and folktales
   - Learning (numbers, letters, colors)
   - Yoga and movement
   - Nature and science
   - Funny stories
   - Classic fairy tales
   Done → goes straight to home screen, library is auto-curated based on age + preferences

No PIN. No account. No email. Onboarding takes under 60 seconds.

SCREENS TO BUILD

1. HOME SCREEN ("[Name]'s Stories")
Layout is a vertically scrolling feed of mixed tile types, ordered by preference relevance:

- TOP: a large featured tile (1 story, full-width, "Today's Pick for [Name]")
- THEN: a horizontal-scroll row labeled "[Name]'s Favorites" (6-8 picture cards based on preferences)
- THEN: a 2x3 grid labeled "More Like This" (cards from related categories)
- THEN: another horizontal-scroll row labeled "New for You" (curated picks outside stated preferences, for discovery)
- THEN: a horizontal-scroll row "Quick Listens" (stories under 5 minutes)
- THEN: a horizontal-scroll row "Long Stories" (stories over 10 minutes)
- THEN: a 2x3 grid "Loved by 3-year-olds" (or whatever age — age-matched)

Tile design:
- Each card is a full-bleed illustration with story title in large rounded font
- Cards are visually varied in size: featured tile is huge (full width),
  horizontal-scroll cards are medium (45% screen width), grid cards are smaller
- Tap any card → goes directly to player (no intermediate screen)

The variety of tile sizes and layouts is intentional — keeps the screen visually
interesting for a toddler while staying organized for the parent who set it up.

2. PLAYER SCREEN
- Full-screen story illustration (dimmed by 30% to reduce blue light)
- Single large play/pause button center-screen
- Small back arrow top-left
- Audio progress bar (thin, kid-can't-easily-scrub)
- Auto-stops at end of story; kid is returned to home

3. SETTINGS (accessible from a small gear icon top-right of home)
No PIN required. Just basic adjustments:
- Update child's name and age
- Update content preferences (re-pick the tiles)
- Sleep timer (auto-stop after 15/30/60 min)
- Volume cap toggle (max 70%)
- Add another child (creates a separate profile to switch between)

SAMPLE CONTENT (mock 20 stories so the home screen feels populated)
Generate 20 stories spread across all 10 preference categories, with mock 30-second
audio loops as placeholders. Mix of durations from 3 min to 15 min. Examples:

Bedtime: The Sleepy Elephant (8m), Moonbeam and Me (6m)
Adventure: The Tiger Who Came to Tea (7m), The Lost Puppy (7m)
Animal: Little Frog's Big Day (5m), The Brave Mouse (6m)
Songs: Twinkle Twinkle and 4 More (12m), Wheels on the Bus Remix (4m)
Mythology: Krishna and the Butter Pot (5m), The Story of Ganesh (6m)
Learning: Counting with Mango Monkey (4m), ABC with Animals (5m)

Funny: The Silly Sandwich (4m), Bananas in the Bathtub (3m)
Classic: Goldilocks (6m), Three Little Pigs (6m), The Hungry Caterpillar (5m)

For each story, generate a cover illustration prompt (warm, hand-drawn,
soft watercolor style — think Eric Carle or Beatrix Potter, not Disney).

PREFERENCE-BASED CURATION LOGIC
- Stories from selected preference categories appear most often (top of feed, larger tiles)
- Stories from age-appropriate range (within +/- 1 year of child's age) are prioritized
- Some discovery content shown (1-2 categories not picked) to expose kid to new themes
- "Today's Pick" rotates daily based on preferences + a random element

VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM
- Aesthetic: warm, soft, hand-drawn. NOT corporate, NOT neon, NOT cartoon-glossy
- Color palette: cream background (#FDF6E3), warm coral (#E8826B),
  forest green (#5B8C5A), soft yellow (#F5C77E), navy (#2D3E5E for text)
- Typography: rounded sans-serif for kids (think "Quicksand" or "Nunito"),
  serif for any longer text (think "Lora")
- Buttons and tiles: minimum 64x64 pixels for kid-facing taps
- Animations: gentle, slow (300-500ms transitions), no aggressive bounces
- No ads, no in-app purchase popups, no notification badges

TECHNICAL PREFERENCES
- React (Next.js if available)
- Mobile-first responsive
- Audio: HTML5 Audio API with mock MP3 placeholders
- State management: useState/useReducer; no Redux
- Mock backend: hardcode the 20 stories as a JSON array
- Onboarding state, child profile, and preferences stored in localStorage
- Usage tracking logged to localStorage (no real analytics yet)

WHAT NOT TO BUILD
- No PIN, no auth, no login of any kind
- No social features
- No video, audio only
- No ads
- No notifications

DELIVERABLE
A working web app I can demo on my phone:
- Onboarding flow takes under 60 seconds end-to-end
- Home screen feels populated, varied, and visually warm
- Tapping any tile plays audio
- Preferences actually shape what's shown on home
- I can switch between child profiles or update preferences without friction
